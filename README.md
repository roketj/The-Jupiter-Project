# The Jupiter Project

## Scope
- Science Fiction space opera spanning multiple realities and timelines

## Setting
- Book 1:  21st century Earth
- Book 2:  Virtual Earth
- Book 3:  Sol System, ~10,000 years in the future

## Characters
- The Professor
- The Temporal Guardian
- The Professor's "Alternates"

## Key Concepts
- Book 1:  Time Travel, Paradoxes
- Book 2:  Simulated Realities
- Book 3:  Simulated People, Artificial Intelligences, The End of the Universe

## Themes
- Personhood
- Love and Sacrifice
- Human Hubris
